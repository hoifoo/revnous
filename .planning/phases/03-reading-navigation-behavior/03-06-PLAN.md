Close out Phase 3 with the checks a headless browser cannot make: does the scroll-spy trigger band FEEL right, does the active pink and rail read correctly at both heading levels, does the disclosure look like a control at real phone and tablet widths, and does the 32px landing offset give enough breathing room.

This plan converts those judgement calls into a written checklist and puts a human in front of them.

Output: a manual QA checklist, a tracked follow-up for the legacy handler, and a human sign-off.

Then write numbered checks, each with a reproduction step, an explicit expected result, and a pass/fail box. Cover exactly these, sourced from the UI contract's Verification Notes:

1. Trigger-band feel — scroll slowly through a long TOC-on post at desktop width and judge whether the active entry changes at a comfortable moment. Expected: the highlight moves as a new section arrives near the top of the reading area, not lagging until mid-screen and not flickering ahead of the section. Record that `TRIGGER_PCT` in `app/javascript/controllers/toc_controller.js` is the single tunable (currently 25, D-01's 20-25% band) and that only its mechanism was verified, not its feel.

2. Active-entry treatment at both levels — confirm the active H2 entry and the active H3 entry both show pink-600 text AND a pink-600 2px left rail, and that neither changes font weight or re-wraps as it activates. Expected: exactly one accented entry at a time, no horizontal shift of the list when activation moves, no vertical twitch.

3. Reserved rail gutter — confirm the 18px left inset on H2 entries and 34px on H3 entries reads as intentional indentation rather than misalignment, and that the H3 entries still sit 16px further right than the H2 entries. This is the UI contract's flagged "confirm at the UI review gate" item and is trivially reversible (two class strings).

4. Disclosure appearance at 375px and 768px — confirm the collapsed control is a single bordered bar above the article roughly 52px tall, with the label `Contents` on the left and the chevron on the right; open it and confirm the chevron rotates 180 degrees and the panel expands in place pushing the article down, with no overlay, no card fill, and no shadow.

5. Reduced motion — turn on the OS-level reduce-motion setting (macOS System Settings, Accessibility, Display, Reduce motion), reload a TOC-on post, and click an entry. Expected: an instant jump with no visible animation, and the chevron rotation on the disclosure also loses its transition.

6. Normal motion — turn the OS setting back off and click an entry. Expected: a single smooth scroll, not two competing scrolls and not a jump-then-scroll.

7. Landing offset breathing room — after any jump, judge whether 32px above the heading reads as enough space. Note that this is Phase 2's `prose-headings:scroll-mt-8` decision and that the site header is `position: relative` and non-sticky, so no header compensation exists. Changing it is a deliberate Phase 2 amendment, not a Phase 3 fix.

8. Long-TOC sidebar containment — on a post with 20-plus headings at desktop width, scroll deep and confirm the sidebar's own list scrolls internally to keep the active entry visible, and that doing so never nudges the article.

9. Keyboard walkthrough — with the keyboard only, Tab to the desktop TOC and confirm each entry shows a visible pink focus ring; then at mobile width Tab to the `Contents` trigger, press Enter to open, Tab into the entries, press Escape and confirm focus returns to the trigger.

10. No-JavaScript degradation — disable JavaScript, reload a TOC-on post, and confirm the disclosure still opens and closes and entry links still jump to their sections. Expected: degraded (no highlight, no focus move, no smooth scroll) but never broken.

11. TOC-off regression sanity — load a post with the TOC off and confirm it looks exactly as it did before this phase: no disclosure, no sidebar, no layout shift, unchanged content width.

Close with a Sign-off section: date, reviewer, overall pass/fail, and a Notes area for any `TRIGGER_PCT` change made during review.

State: `app/views/shared/_navigation.html.erb` binds a global smooth-scroll click handler to every `a[href*="#"]` inside a `DOMContentLoaded` listener; it never rebinds after a Turbo Drive navigation, does not respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and does not move focus. Phase 3 deliberately did NOT fix it — the capture-phase `stopPropagation()` in `toc_controller.js` neutralises it for TOC and permalink clicks with zero edits to that file, and editing it would invalidate all five full-page golden masters because it renders on every page. Record the candidate fixes (scope the selector with a `:not(...)` exclusion, or move the binding to `turbo:load`) and note that this is a separate change with a site-wide blast radius requiring its own golden-master review.

Do NOT edit `app/views/shared/_navigation.html.erb` in this plan.
